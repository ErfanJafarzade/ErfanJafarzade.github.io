Research

AI Input & Language Evolution

My primary research question asks whether generative AI input into early language environments is suppressing the compositional innovation historically driven by gaps in children's linguistic input. Framed through iterated learning theory (Kirby, Griffiths & Smith, 2014), the methodology involves Bayesian agent simulation across 500–1,000 generations and corpus analysis of child-directed speech from 1990 to present.

Iterated learning Bayesian simulation Corpus analysis Language evolution Generative AI

Translation Studies as Cognitive Research

My undergraduate training connects directly to masked translation priming, cross-language lexical access, and morphological awareness. Being a native speaker of Persian and Azeri — both structurally distant from English — provides firsthand grounding in interlingual transfer, not just theoretical familiarity.
